# Sales Partner Ford — Dealer website POC

Interactive HTML proof-of-concept for an authorised **Ford & Ford Pro** dealer with **2 locations**:
- Showroom Berceni (cars);
- Ford Pro Center Chitila (commercial vehicles, fleets).

**Structure:** the same as the OMODA & JAECOO POC, via the Hyundai single-brand version: mega menu, prices page, configurator, stock, offers, service, locations, legal.

**Look:** Ford C&I as seen on ford.ro in 2026:
- white header with the blue oval;
- Ford Blue `#1700F4` pill buttons;
- light-grey `#F1F1F1` surfaces;
- Ford navy `#00095B` bands and footer;
- **Inter** (OFL) in place of the proprietary Ford Antenna font.

> **Demo only.** Dealer data, people, stock, VINs and dealer-accessory prices are fictional.
> Models, list prices, promo prices, series, engines, colours, interiors and factory options come from **ford.ro** (Ford România, October 2026).
> Images and video are hot-linked from the Ford media servers (`gpas-cache.ford.com`, `ford.ro`, `ford.eu`).

## What is new compared with the other brands

1. **Full configurator for 6 models**, built like the ford.ro one. Data is in `assets/js/config-data.js`, generated by `gen.py` from the ford.ro catalogue.
   - Covered models: Puma, Kuga, Capri, Explorer, Transit Custom and Ranger.
   - Steps: Serie → (Caroserie, Transit Custom only) → Motorizare → Exterior → Interior → Opționale → Rezumat.
   - **Price breakdown**, as on ford.ro:

     | Line | Shown as |
     |---|---|
     | List price | amount |
     | Options | amount |
     | Rabla de la Ford | − amount |
     | Customer advantage | − amount |
     | Promotional price | amount |
     | **Dealer accessories** | amount |
     | **Sales Partner dealer price** | final amount |

   - **Dealer accessories and services:** wallbox, Sortimo racking, Ford Protect, winter wheels. They are added in the Opționale step. This is the dealer's own layer on top of the Ford catalogue.
   - Series comparison, an image gallery per colour, a delivery estimate or a "N în stoc" match, finance, and a configuration code.
   - The lead is sent as `type=configuration`.
2. **Private / business toggle.** It sits in the top bar, the drawer and the configurator, and is also available as `?client=business`.
   - All prices are stored with VAT. Commercial vehicles, which Ford lists without VAT, are stored ×1.21.
   - In business mode every price is shown **without VAT** and **Rabla de la Ford is removed** (it is for private buyers only).
3. **Ford Pro page** (`ford-pro.html`): commercial range, why Ford Pro, fleet quote form, Ford Pro Center card.
4. **Video hero** (Ford range film from ford.eu) and **range tabs**: Autoturisme / Electrificate / Comerciale & Pick-up, in both the home page and the mega menu.
5. **Other models** (Mach-E, Mustang, Bronco, Tourneo, Transit, Transit Courier/Connect/City, Raptor, Transit Bus):
   - they show the list price and promotional price "de la", taken from the ford.ro range pages;
   - the configurator becomes a quote request for them.

## About the configurator data (please read)

- Prices were read from the ford.ro configurator one series and one engine at a time.
- If a combination's full breakdown could not be read, only the promotional price shown in the bottom bar was available. In that case:
  - Rabla and customer advantage are taken from the same engine in another series;
  - the version is marked **"estimat"** in the configurator and on the prices page.
- Transit Custom Sport and Ranger Platinum / MS-RT have the most estimates.
- Some series highlights are placeholders, until the importer catalogue is connected:
  - Kuga ST-Line, ST-Line X and Active X;
  - Transit Custom Limited and Sport;
  - Ranger Limited, Platinum and MS-RT.
- **Production:** `SP.CFG` comes from Sales Partner (`GET /api/catalog/{model}`), fed from the importer catalogue. It is not scraped.
- **Workleto sites** (ford.carbenta.com, fordroadhill.ro): they use the same URL-step flow and the same Ford gpas images.
  - They were used only as a cross-check of the step order.
  - No content, prices or images were taken from them.

## Run it

- **Locally:** open `index.html`. You need internet access for the Ford images and the video.
- **Vercel:**
  - Repo `xeatra/dealerwebsite`.
  - Root Directory `ford`, Framework Other, no build command.
  - `vercel.json` sends `noindex`; `robots.txt` blocks crawlers.
- **Language:** RO / EN toggle, or `?lang=en`.

## Integration points (CakePHP 4 / Sales Partner APIs)

| UI | Endpoint |
|---|---|
| Lead forms, configurator, fleet form | `POST /api/leads` (type, model, version or configuration code, private/business) |
| Configurator catalogue | `GET /api/catalog/{model}` (series, engines, colours, interiors, options, price breakdown) |
| Stock | `GET /api/stock` |
| Service booking | `POST /api/service-bookings` |
| Offers / news | SP Campaigns / CMS |
